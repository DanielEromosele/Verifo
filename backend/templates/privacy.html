Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Verifo ("we", "us") handles personal information that flows through our document verification service. We designed Verifo for institutions that verify credentials and documents they issued, and this policy reflects that purpose.

1. What we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization, and role when you create or are invited to a workspace.
  • Documents you upload — the files submitted for verification, reference copies your organization publishes, and the extracted fields, scores, and decisions produced from them.
  • Usage and audit events — logins, uploads, decisions, configuration changes, and API activity, which we record to keep an accurate audit trail.
  • Technical metadata — timestamps, IP addresses, and API key activity for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use it

  • To run the verification service and protect its integrity.
  • To render verdicts, produce the audit log, and let your organization review decisions.
  • To communicate with you about your workspace, security, and changes to the service.
  • To keep the platform safe and prevent misuse.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use the contents of verified documents to advertise to you.
  • We do not disclose uploaded documents to third parties except as required to operate the service, comply with law, or with your organization's instruction.

4. Retention

Documents, references, and audit records are retained as long as your organization's workspace exists, so verifications stay auditable. Your organization controls this data; contact an administrator in your workspace to request deletion. We may keep minimal access logs for a reasonable period to secure the platform.

5. How we protect it

Communications are encrypted in transit. Access is scoped by organization and role, sessions are bearer-token based, and API access uses keyed credentials. Decisions and configuration changes are written to an append-only audit log so that activity is accountable.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Because we are a processor for your organization, please raise requests through your organization's administrator first.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to:
